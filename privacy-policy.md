# Privacy Policy - Deck Delve

*Last updated: 19.8.2026 | Effective for version 0.1 onward*

This privacy policy explains what data Deck Delve ("the game") collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled. Deck Delve is developed by ppodd, a sole proprietor based in Switzerland.

## 1. What we collect

Deck Delve uses **GameAnalytics** to collect gameplay data that helps us understand how the game is played and improve it over time. Specifically, this may include:

- **Gameplay events** - actions such as card plays, deck choices, level progress, deaths, and run outcomes
- **Session data** - session length, number of sessions, time between sessions
- **Performance metrics** - frames per second (FPS) and related technical performance data
- **Device and platform information** - operating system, hardware category, and a persistent, anonymous device or installation identifier assigned by GameAnalytics (used to distinguish unique players and sessions)

We do **not** collect names, email addresses, payment information, or any data that directly identifies you as an individual. All data is tied to an anonymous identifier, not to your real-world identity.

## 2. Why we collect it

This data is used solely to:

- Understand how players engage with game systems and content
- Identify and fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues
- Balance difficulty and progression based on aggregate player behavior
- Guide future development priorities for Deck Delve

We do not use this data for advertising, and it is not sold to third parties.

## 3. Who we share it with

Gameplay data is processed by our analytics provider, **GameAnalytics ApS** (Denmark), which may act as an independent data controller for certain technical/device data under its own terms. You can read GameAnalytics' privacy policy here: [gameanalytics.com/privacy](https://gameanalytics.com/privacy).

We do not share your data with any other third party beyond what is necessary to operate GameAnalytics' service.

## 4. Legal basis and your consent

Where required by law (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR), we rely on your **consent** as the legal basis for collecting this data. You will be asked to accept or decline analytics collection the first time you launch the game. If you decline, no gameplay data is sent to GameAnalytics, and the game remains fully playable.

You may withdraw consent at any time via the in-game settings menu (*Settings -> Game Analytics*).

## 5. Data retention

Data collected via GameAnalytics is retained according to GameAnalytics' standard retention policy, currently 12 months. After this period, data is automatically deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Your rights

If GDPR or a similar data protection law applies to you, you have the right to:

- Request access to data associated with you (where identifiable)
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent to data collection at any time
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because data is tied to an anonymous device identifier rather than your name or account, we may not always be able to locate a specific record without your device/installation ID. Contact us using the details below and we will assist where possible.

## 7. Children's privacy

Deck Delve is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Persistent identifiers collected by GameAnalytics are not used for advertising or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as Deck Delve evolves. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and where appropriate, flagged in-game.
